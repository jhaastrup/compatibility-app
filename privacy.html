Who we are

This website is run by Compatible. We decide how your information is used, which makes us the “data controller”. For anything about your data, email [privacy email].

Each quiz is made by an individual creator. When you leave your details for a creator, they receive them as a private person, and how they use them to contact you is between you and them.

What we collect

If you create a quiz

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in links and, if you switch it on, emails about new matches.
  • Your confirmation that you’re 18 or older, and when you gave it.
  • Your quiz: the name you show, your link, your questions, the answers you’d accept, how much each matters, your age range and your match threshold.

If you take a quiz

  • Your age and your answers, plus your score and whether you matched.
  • A scrambled code made from your network address and the quiz, so we can limit each network to 3 tries per quiz per day. The code can’t be turned back into your address, and we don’t store the address itself.

If you match and agree to share

  • Your first name, the contact you choose (an Instagram or X handle, a WhatsApp number or an email address, depending on what the creator accepts), any note you write, and when you agreed.
  • If you share a social media handle, the creator can view your public profile on that platform.

What our providers see automatically

Like any website, the services that deliver these pages (listed under Who can see it) receive your network address and basic browser details when you load a page.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To run quizzes and work out matches, based on your consent. Creators agree when they sign up. Visitors agree when they start a quiz, and again, separately, before sharing contact details.
  • To pass your contact to the creator you matched with, based on the consent you give with the tick box.
  • To keep the service safe (limiting repeat attempts, keeping each creator’s data separate, stopping misuse), based on our legitimate interest in running a secure service.
  • To send sign-in and match emails to creators who asked for them.

We don’t use your information for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t use it to profile you beyond the quiz you chose to take.

Personal topics in quizzes

Some quizzes ask about personal things such as faith. Under Nigerian data protection law, information about religious beliefs is treated as sensitive. We only use these answers, with your consent, to check whether you and that quiz’s creator are compatible. If you’d rather not answer a quiz’s questions, please don’t start it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to delete your answers.

How matching works

Matching is automatic. When you finish, our system compares your answers with the answers the creator said they’d accept, weighs each question by how much it matters to them, and checks your age is in their range. No person reviews it. The only effect of the result is whether you’re offered the chance to leave your contact details. If you want to know more about how a result was reached, or to challenge it, email us.

Who can see it

  • The quiz creator can see the answers and ages submitted to their own quiz, without names or contact details. If you matched and agreed to share, they also see your name, contact, note and answers. Creators never see anything from other creators’ quizzes.
  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the fonts and some of the code that pages load.
  • Our email provider sends sign-in links and match emails to creators.
  • Instagram, X and WhatsApp: if a creator opens your profile or a chat with you, that happens on those platforms, under their own privacy policies.

We only share information with someone else if the law requires it or it’s needed to protect someone’s safety.

Where it’s stored

Our providers store and process data outside Nigeria. The database is hosted by Supabase in Ireland (EU), and Netlify, Google and jsDelivr serve pages from data centres around the world. We use established providers that protect data with security safeguards and contractual commitments.

Storage in your browser

We don’t use cookies for advertising or tracking, and we don’t use analytics. Your browser’s own storage keeps creators signed in, and remembers whether you’ve already taken a quiz so you aren’t asked again. Clearing your browser data removes it.

How long we keep it

  • Contact details from a match are kept until the creator deletes the match or their quiz, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Quiz answers are kept for as long as the quiz exists, so the creator’s results stay accurate. Deleting a quiz deletes every answer and match connected to it.
  • Creator accounts are kept until you ask us to delete yours.

Security

All connections are encrypted. Database rules mean a creator can only read their own quiz’s data, visitors can’t read anyone’s data, and a creator’s preferred answers never leave the server. Contact details can only be left after a genuine match, and they have to be claimed within two hours. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold data about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that’s wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to it;
  • give you your data in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent (this doesn’t affect anything done before you withdrew).

Email [privacy email]. Because taking a quiz is anonymous, tell us which quiz it was, roughly when, and the name and contact you left (if any), so we can find your data. We may ask you to confirm it’s you. We aim to respond within 30 days.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. If you live outside Nigeria, you can also contact the data protection authority where you live.

Age

This service is only for people aged 18 and over. Both creators and visitors confirm their age, and the system turns away anyone who says they’re younger. If we learn we hold data from someone under 18, we delete it.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll also let creators know by email.
